# Food Delivery Operations & Customer Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing 15,000 food delivery orders to understand delivery performance, operational efficiency, and customer behavior.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to identify patterns in delivery operations and customer behavior using PostgreSQL and Tableau.

## Key Questions

- How does traffic affect delivery time?
- How does delivery distance affect delivery time?
- Which factors are associated with poor delivery efficiency?
- How do premium customers differ in spending?
- How are orders distributed across customer age groups?
- What are the cancellation and refund rates?

## Key Findings

- Higher traffic levels are associated with longer delivery times.
- Deliveries above 15 km have an average delivery time of approximately 113 minutes.
- Poor-efficiency deliveries show substantially higher average delivery times than excellent-efficiency deliveries.
- Premium customers have higher average spending than non-premium customers.
- Young and middle-age customers account for a large share of orders.
- The overall cancellation rate is 13.35%.
- The overall refund rate is 4.12%.
